04 · Hack on it

A handful of decorators, your fleet at your fingertips.

Free Motion is a Python package. Plug in your Telegram token, point it at a MAVLink-speaking drone, and register a handler. Everything else — chat history, vision passes, return-to-home — is taken care of.

Want a different LLM? Swap it out. Want voice instead of text? Add a transcriber. It's MIT — go nuts.

scout.py python 
from freemotion import Agent, Drone

agent = Agent.from_telegram("$BOT_TOKEN")
drone = Drone.connect("/dev/ttyUSB0")

@agent.command("scout")
async def scout(ctx, area: str):
    # lobster, take the wheel
    await drone.takeoff(altitude=18)

    async for frame in drone.grid_search(area):
        finding = await agent.vision(frame)
        if finding.interesting:
            await ctx.reply(frame, caption=finding.caption)

    await drone.return_home()
    return "✓ rtb · battery 62%"

agent.run()  # 🦞 ✈️

06 · FAQ
Is this legal where I live?
Probably partly, probably not entirely. Drone laws vary by country and altitude. Free Motion doesn't override your flight controller's geofence or no-fly zones — it sits on top of MAVLink, so anything your drone refuses to do, the lobster won't make it do either. Read your local rules.
Why a lobster?
Free Motion is the sister project to OpenClaw. OpenClaw started the lobster. We just gave it wings.
Does it work without internet?
Mostly. Telegram needs a connection at your end and at the Pi's end — a hotspot is fine. The mission and vision models can run fully local with a small quantized model, or you can point them at a hosted endpoint if you'd rather burn API credits than RAM.
What models does it use?
By default a small open-weights LLM for planning and a tiny VLM for vision — both quantized to fit on a Pi. Anything that exposes a chat-completions API slots in via config.yaml. It's all swappable.
Can I use a webcam instead of a real drone?
Yes. There's a --sim flag that wires the vision model to a USB webcam and stubs out flight commands. Great for hacking on the prompts before you risk a propeller.
How do I contribute?
Open an issue, send a PR, or just tell us what broke. The repo has a HACKING.md with the architecture map and where to start.
